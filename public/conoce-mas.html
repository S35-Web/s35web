Discover how laboratory-developed formulas transform efficiency and quality in construction.

The problem with traditional on-site mixing

When materials are mixed directly on site, multiple factors can compromise the final product quality:

Inconsistent proportions

Manual measurements can vary significantly between workers and workdays.

Variable environmental conditions

Humidity, temperature, and water quality can unpredictably affect the final result.

Limited working time

On-site mixed products have a shorter shelf life, generating waste.

Difficult quality control

It is complex to guarantee that every batch has the same characteristics and performance.

The solution: Laboratory pre-formulated products

S-35 products are formulated under controlled laboratory conditions, where every variable can be measured, controlled, and optimized for consistent, superior results.

Impact on the construction industry

Adopting pre-formulated products is transforming the construction industry, generating tangible benefits:

Cleaner job sites

Less dust, less waste, and a healthier work environment for crews.

Greater efficiency

Optimized processes that reduce construction time and operating costs.

Precise formulas

Predictable, consistent results that meet the most demanding quality standards.

Sustainability

Reduced environmental impact through less waste and more efficient processes.
